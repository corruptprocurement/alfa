How to read this atlas

Evidence before inference.

This demo shows 200 source-level connection records from the Bulgarian public-procurement knowledge graph. A connection is an extracted relationship with a supporting excerpt; it is not a separate corruption case.

Where the sample comes from

The selection takes the first 200 records, in export order, from production batches 017 onward in the 2024–February 2025 graph: 66 from batch 017, 104 from batch 018 and 30 from batch 019. It covers 22 stories and 138 existing entity identifiers. The last story may be only partly represented. This is an illustrative, non-random sample, not a representative estimate.

The wider project draws on 79,950 publications from Sensika media-monitoring exports. The researcher reports that the software monitors over 500 Bulgarian media outlets of different sizes. Media coverage, export completeness and coding coverage differ.

Translation and identity

Connection labels, entity descriptions, headlines, structured details and all 154 distinct evidence excerpts, and coding notes are translated into English. Personal and company names are transliterated where appropriate. These are AI-assisted reading translations, not certified translations. The original Bulgarian excerpts, names, headlines and coding notes are retained for comparison. Loaded wording belongs to the cited source.

The original graph’s entity identifiers are retained. Earlier name-based merges have not been revalidated for this demo; matching names do not establish identity. Institutional names are English reading labels, not a claim to official nomenclature.

What the statuses mean

  • Reported: stated by the source; not independently verified here.
  • Claimed: attributed allegation or assertion. Read the named claimant.
  • Rumour: rumour or anonymous-source claim.
  • Official finding: coded as a finding by an official body; not necessarily a final conviction. Read the excerpt and any subsequent outcome.
  • Denied: the relationship or assertion is recorded as denied.

Dashed network lines mark claims, rumours and denials. Node colours identify entity types, not risk or guilt. Repeated relationships can be recorded in different articles. No risk score is calculated.

Purpose and limits

The graph supports research into procurement networks and, after identity checks and further validation, preventive screening in public systems. It should guide examination of evidence, not automatically determine sanctions or exclusion. Source links open outlet homepages because article-level URLs were absent from the export.
